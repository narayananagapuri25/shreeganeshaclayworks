CLAY PRODUCTS IN MANCHERIAL

Clay diyas, clay pots and
traditional pottery.

Looking for clay diyas or clay pots in Mancherial? Shree Ganesha Clay Works is a local clay-products shop in Naspur, Mancherial, Telangana. Explore the listed collections and contact the store to check current designs, sizes, rates and availability.

Clay diyas and traditional lamps

The catalogue includes numbered clay diyas, special diya designs, plain agul and stand agul, plus B/Simini and C/Simini lamps. The rate guide shows single-piece and dozen rates for diya items. Product photographs are reference images; ask the shop to confirm current designs and stock.

Explore clay diyas and rates→

Clay pots and cooking vessels

The listed clayware includes cooking vessels from ½ litre to 5 litres, water pots in 8, 12, 15 and 20 litre sizes, and Ranjan. The supplied rate guide lists cooking vessels at ₹100–₹300 and water pots at ₹150–₹300. Confirm today's price and availability with the shop.
